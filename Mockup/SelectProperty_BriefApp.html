How it works

Every brief — Quick or Full — moves through a 4-stage gate. Stages unlock in order. Todoist tasks fire only after full approval.

1 · Draft
Brief created by content exec / campaign manager
2 · Understanding confirmation
Author sends interpretation for requester sign-off
3 · Content review (2 rounds)
Round 1 feedback. Round 2 sign-off only
4 · Final approval + Todoist
HoM (+ CEO if triggered). Tasks push to Todoist
